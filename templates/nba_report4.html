📘 Formula

Final CO Attainment

= (0.8 × Direct CO Attainment)

+ (0.2 × Indirect CO Attainment)

🧮 Specimen Calculation

Example

Direct CO Attainment   = 82%

Indirect CO Attainment = 75%

Final CO Attainment

= (0.8 × 82)

+ (0.2 × 75)

= 65.6 + 15

= 80.6%

📝 Interpretation

Final CO Attainment combines the Direct CO Attainment (80% weightage) and the Indirect CO Attainment (20% weightage). This weighted value represents the overall attainment of each Course Outcome and is used for NBA accreditation analysis and continuous quality improvement.
