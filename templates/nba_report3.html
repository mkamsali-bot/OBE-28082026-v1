📘 Formula

Indirect CO Attainment
Indirect CO Attainment (%) = Course Exit Survey Percentage
Attainment Level Criteria
Percentage Level
< 60% Level 1
60% – 69.99% Level 2
≥ 70% Level 3

🧮 Specimen Calculation

Example

Course Exit Survey Percentage = 82%

Since 82% ≥ 70%

Indirect CO Attainment Level = 3

📝 Interpretation

Indirect CO attainment is determined from the Course Exit Survey completed by students at the end of the course. The survey percentage is converted into an attainment level using predefined thresholds. This reflects students’ perception of how well each Course Outcome has been achieved.
