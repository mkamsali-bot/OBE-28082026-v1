Report Summary

This report presents the Indirect Contribution of Course Outcomes (COs) towards Program Outcomes (POs) and Program Specific Outcomes (PSOs).

  • Indirect CO Attainment Levels are obtained from Course Exit Survey / Indirect Assessment.
  • The contribution is computed using the CO–PO/PSO Mapping Strength.
  • Higher contribution values indicate stronger support towards achieving the corresponding PO or PSO.
