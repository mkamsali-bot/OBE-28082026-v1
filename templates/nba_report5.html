📘 Formula

CO–PO / PSO Mapping

Faculty maps every Course Outcome (CO)
to each PO and PSO.

Mapping Levels

0 = No Mapping
1 = Low
2 = Medium
3 = High

Example

These values represent the strength
of correlation between the Course
Outcome and Program Outcomes.

📝 Interpretation

CO–PO/PSO mapping establishes the relationship between Course Outcomes and the Program Outcomes/Program Specific Outcomes. The mapping strength (0–3) is determined by the course instructor and forms the basis for calculating direct and indirect PO attainment in the subsequent NBA reports.
