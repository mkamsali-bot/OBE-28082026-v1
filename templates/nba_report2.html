Attainment Level Criteria

🧮 Specimen Calculation

Example

Average CO1 Score = 19.48

Maximum CO Marks = 25

Direct CO Attainment (%)

= (19.48 / 25) × 100

= 77.92 %

Attainment Level = 3

📝 Interpretation

Direct CO attainment is calculated using the average marks obtained by students for each Course Outcome. The attainment percentage is converted into an attainment level based on predefined thresholds. Higher attainment levels indicate better achievement of the corresponding Course Outcome.
